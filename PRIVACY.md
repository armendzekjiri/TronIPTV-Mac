# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

TronIPTV for Mac (“the app”) is a media player. It does not have accounts and does not collect, sell or share
personal data.

## What the app stores

Everything stays on your Mac:

- The playlists you add (names, server or playlist addresses, usernames, passwords, optional guide URL and User-Agent),
  your favorites, your watch history, and your settings.
- A saved copy of each playlist's library and downloaded TV guides, and cached channel logos and posters.

Passwords, playlist addresses and saved libraries are encrypted with a key that is stored in your macOS Keychain.
Deleting the app's data or choosing **Settings → Privacy → Clear Cache** removes the cached data.

## What the app sends over the network

The app connects only to:

- the IPTV providers, playlist addresses, guide addresses and image addresses that **you** enter or that your
  playlists contain, to load channels, guides, logos and streams; and
- GitHub (`api.github.com`), only when you choose **Check for Updates…** in the version downloaded from GitHub.
  The Mac App Store version has no update check.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting built into the app. Apple may provide crash
reports to the developer if you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in macOS settings.

## Content

The app does not provide, host or link to any channels, streams or playlists. You are responsible for the sources
you add.
